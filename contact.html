Frequently Addressed Topics

Does Aurelle sell watches or fine jewelry directly?

No. Aurelle is an independent educational guide, technical reference, and collector tool. We do not participate in commercial transactions or accept sponsored product placement.

How are your specifications verified?

All specs are validated against official manufacture calibre sheets, GIA grading lab reports, and ISO/NIHS testing standards.
