Equipment & Preservation Manual

Essential Equipment & Care Guide

Preserving fine jewelry and precision horology requires specialized instruments. Below is our curated curriculum on optical inspection, micro-tooling, ultrasonic cleaning, and climate-controlled storage.

1. Jewelry Care & Optics 2. Watchmaker Tooling 3. Storage & Travel Protection
Discipline I

Jewelry Care & Gemological Diagnostics

Optical Diagnostic

10x Achromatic Triplet Loupe

The universal standard for inspecting diamond cut symmetry, culets, GIA girdle laser inscriptions, and gemstone clarity inclusions.

Why It Matters: Magnifies without optical color distortion or edge blurring.
Who Needs It: Serious buyers, gemstone appraisers, vintage jewelry collectors.
How To Use: Anchor against orbital eye bone; bring jewel to ~1 inch distance.
View Full Technical Spec
Cleaning & Decontamination

Ultrasonic Wave Bath (42 kHz)

Employs microscopic cavitation bubbles to lift body lotions, grime, and oils from hard-to-reach pavé settings and ring under-galleries.

Why It Matters: Restores maximum diamond brilliance and light transmission.
Warning: NEVER clean emeralds, opals, pearls, or tanzanites in ultrasonic baths.
Ideal For: Platinum, 18k solid gold, diamonds, sapphires, and rubies.

Discipline II

Horological Tooling & Spring-Bar Instruments

Strap & Bracelet Maintenance

Swiss Spring-Bar Instrument

Hardened high-carbon tool steel micro-fork engineered to safely compress spring bars between lugs without marring brushed or mirror-polished chamfers.

Why It Matters: Prevents gouging the underside of luxury watch lugs.
Technique: Apply painter's masking tape to lug backs prior to tool insertion.
View Full Technical Spec
Kinetic Preservation

Geneva Quad Programmable Winder

Maintains perpetual calendar complications, moon phases, and automatic rotors in calibrated motion with intermittent, non-stressing TPD rotations.

Why It Matters: Eliminates the laborious process of resetting intricate calendar wheels.
TPD Standard: 650–800 Turns Per Day bi-directional suits 95% of Swiss calibres.

Discipline III

Collector Storage & Travel Protection

Travel Protection

Verona Prism 3-Watch Travel Roll

Hexagonal crush-proof architectural construction with sliding rail cushions, ensuring timepieces never touch or abrade during transit.

Key Feature: Flat base prevents rolling on nightstands; deep microfiber lining.

Climate & Heirloom Storage

LusterLoc™ Treated Jewelry Casket

Absorbs hostile atmospheric sulfur gases known to oxidize sterling silver and yellow gold alloys, preserving mirror polish for up to 35 years.

Storage Rule: Always separate diamonds from softer pearls to prevent surface scratches.
